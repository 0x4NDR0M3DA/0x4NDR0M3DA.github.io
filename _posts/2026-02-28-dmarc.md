Hey,

a quick one about DMARC role in OSINT/email enum. 

If you're not familiar with it, I highly recommend reading [this MXToolbox article](https://mxtoolbox.com/dmarc/details/what-is-dmarc).

During DNS records sweeps for some domains, I found that DMARC record can include an actual e-mail address that isn't a generic
"dmarcreporting@somemailprovider.com".

If configured, you can query for DMARC record with **dig** and specifying **TXT** record. As an example:

**dig _dmarc.example.com txt**
